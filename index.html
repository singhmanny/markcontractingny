What we do

From the first fence panel to the last load of debris, one crew handles the job. We pull the permits, coordinate with the city, and keep the site safe for neighbors and the public.

Demolition

Full and partial demolition of houses, garages and commercial buildings, including emergency demolition of unsafe structures on short notice.

Construction fencing

Temporary plywood and chain-link site fencing installed to plan and to city requirements, so your permits and inspections go smoothly.

Masonry

Brick, block and concrete work: walls, foundations, steps and repairs, done to last and finished cleanly.

General construction

Site work and general construction for owners, developers and contractors, from clearing the lot to getting it ready to build.

How a job runs

No surprises. You know what happens next at every stage.

  1. Call or send the details

    Tell us the address and what needs to happen. We reply the same or next business day.

  2. Site visit and quote

    We walk the site, check what permits are needed and give you a written price.

  3. Permits and setup

    We file with the city, install fencing and protection, and schedule the crew.

  4. Work and cleanup

    We do the job, haul away debris and leave the site clean and ready for what comes next.
